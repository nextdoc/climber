# Community

[Join the Apex Climber Slack workspace](https://join.slack.com/t/apexclimber/shared_invite/zt-4eydcxy1j-vBVchQjCJU1J5nv1~bsF_Q)

The Slack workspace is the place to ask questions, share ascents and their reports, and discuss ideas for climber.

## Guidelines

1. Be respectful. Critique ideas and code, not people.
2. Stay on topic. Keep discussion related to climber, Apex, and agent-driven code improvement.
3. Search before asking. Check the [README](../README.md) and the [docs](.) first, then ask with enough detail to reproduce the problem: the command you ran, the config, and the output.
4. Share what you learn. Reports, scores, costs and failed ascents all help others.
5. Protect private data. Never post org credentials, access tokens, customer data or code you aren't allowed to share.
6. No spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.
7. Report bugs and feature requests as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/nextdoc/climb-your-apex/issues) so they are tracked.
8. Help others. Answer questions when you can.

Moderators may remove messages or members that break these guidelines.
